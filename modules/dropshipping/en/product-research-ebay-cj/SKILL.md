# Product Research — eBay + CJ, No Ad Budget

Adapted from a trend-scouting guide written for ad-driven Shopify jewelry sales in Denmark. Three
assumptions in the original do not hold here, and correcting them changes the method rather than just
the vocabulary:

| Original assumption | Reality here | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Paid ads create demand | **No ad budget** | You *harvest* existing search demand, you do not create it |
| Social engagement is the primary signal | **Terapeak gives real completed sales** | Social drops to corroboration; sold data leads |
| Get in early on emerging trends | eBay rewards proven, searchable demand | **Emerging is the riskier stage for you, not the safer one** |

The original also has no margin dimension at all — fatal for dropshipping, where a trending product with
no spread is a way to lose money efficiently. It is added below and weighted heavily.

## 1. Scoring — 0 to 100

Re-weighted for this operator. Score every candidate across six dimensions.

| Dimension | Weight | How to score |
|---|---|---|
| **Proven sold volume** (Terapeak, 90 days) | 0–30 | Steady weekly sales across multiple sellers = 30 · Regular but thin = 20 · Sporadic = 10 · Single spike = 0 · No sold data = **reject** |
| **Margin viability** | 0–20 | ≥$15 net at a $25–60 sale price = 20 · $10–15 = 14 · $7–10 = 7 · <$7 = **reject** |
| **eBay saturation** | 0–15 | Few sellers, none dominant = 15 · Moderate = 10 · Several with 1000+ feedback = 5 · Dominated by one giant = 0 |
| **CJ US-warehouse stock** | 0–15 | In US warehouse, healthy stock = 15 · US but thin = 8 · **China-only = reject** |
| **Defect risk** (competitor negative feedback) | 0–10 | No recurring complaints = 10 · Isolated = 6 · Recurring same defect = **reject** |
| **Seasonality** | 0–10 | Year-round = 10 · Peak approaching = 8 · Peak now = 5 · Off-season = 2 |

**Reading the total**

- **75–100** — Strong. Take it to the category-eligibility gate.
- **55–74** — Workable. Usually a margin or saturation problem; check whether a bundle or variant fixes it.
- **35–54** — Weak. Only with a genuine differentiator, which a beginner rarely has.
- **Under 35** — Pass.

Note the four hard rejects. They are rejects, not low scores: no sold data, sub-$7 net, China-only
stock, and recurring defect complaints each independently kill a candidate however well it scores
elsewhere.

## 2. Trend lifecycle — read it backwards

The original guide says enter EMERGING for first-mover advantage. **That advice belongs to ad-driven
selling, where you pay to manufacture demand.** With no ad budget you depend on buyers already
searching eBay for the thing. So:

| Stage | Ad-driven verdict | **Your verdict** |
|---|---|---|
| Emerging | Best entry | **Avoid** — nobody is searching for it on eBay yet |
| Rising | Prime | **Good** — search demand exists and is growing |
| Peak | Ride it | **Best** — maximum search volume; compete on listing quality, not novelty |
| Declining | Exit | Avoid |

You are not trying to be early. You are trying to be *findable* against demand that already exists.
Pure dropshipping carries no inventory, so the usual penalty for entering at peak — dead stock — does
not apply to you.

## 3. Sources, in priority order

### Primary — Terapeak (free with the seller account)

`Seller Hub → Research → Product Research`. Three years of **completed sales**, including real accepted
Best Offer prices that never show in ordinary search. Everything else on this list is corroboration.

For each candidate record: units sold in 90 days, average sold price, price spread, number of active
sellers, sell-through rate.

### Primary — competitor negative feedback

eBay seller feedback is public. Find sellers of the same item and read their negative and neutral
comments. This is simultaneously the defect-risk score *and* the substitute for buying a sample — it
reads across hundreds of units rather than one. Buyers name defects plainly: "arrived broken",
"smaller than pictured", "stopped working".

### Secondary — corroboration only

- **Google Trends** — region **United States**, 12-month view. Confirms the demand is real and not
  decaying. Rising or stable, never post-spike collapse.
- **TikTok Creative Center** and **Meta Ad Library** — free. Show what angles others sell on and how
  long their ads run. Useful for listing copy; **not** evidence of eBay demand.
- **Pinterest** — high purchase intent and long lead time, but skews to planned/aesthetic purchases.
  Weak signal for eBay.

**Confidence rule:** Terapeak sold volume + Google Trends stable = act. Social buzz with no Terapeak
sold data = **ignore it**. On eBay, no completed sales means no demand, whatever TikTok shows.

## 4. Hard screens — apply before scoring

Run these first; they are cheap and eliminate most candidates.

1. **Sourcing compliance** — CJ is a wholesale supplier, so CJ → eBay is permitted. **Never source by
   buying from another retailer or marketplace (Amazon, Walmart, AliExpress retail) to ship to the
   buyer.** That is retail arbitrage, which eBay bans; penalty is account termination. Reject any
   candidate whose only viable source is retail.
2. **Shariah product filter** — exclude alcohol-related, gambling, adult, pork or ingestible, riba
   instruments, idols and talismans, counterfeits. Precious metals and currency are excluded by the
   salam structure itself.
3. **Physical** — under 500 g, not fragile, not size-dependent, no service life, no electronics.
4. **Price band** — target $25–60.

## 5. Category eligibility — before any production work

Jordan is on eBay's HiPO covered-country list, so a seller with no history is blocked from a set of
categories eBay deliberately does not publish. Create an **unpublished draft listing** in the candidate's
category and see whether an ineligibility warning appears. If blocked, move to the next candidate;
never attempt to work around it. Run this before writing copy or producing images.

## 6. Cadence for 7–15 h/week

The original guide's weekly/bi-weekly/monthly/quarterly rhythm assumes an ongoing brand operation.
Compressed for a single operator finding a first product:

| When | ~Time | Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekly** | 45–60 min | Terapeak on 5–10 candidates; log units sold, average price, seller count, sell-through |
| **Weekly** | 20 min | Read competitor negative feedback for the current shortlist |
| **Bi-weekly** | 20 min | Google Trends check on the shortlist; drop anything decaying |
| **Monthly** | 45 min | Score everything surviving; take the top candidate to the eligibility gate |

Once a product is live, this shrinks to a monthly scan — listing quality and fulfilment matter more
than finding the next product.

## 6b. Working across devices — Terapeak lives on the operator's machine

Terapeak runs inside the operator's eBay Seller Hub. **The assistant cannot reach it**: there is no
eBay MCP, and eBay.com blocks automated fetching. So the loop is manual by necessity:

```
operator reads Terapeak  →  fills the capture CSV  →  pastes/uploads it here
                          →  assistant scores, ranks, flags rejects
                          →  operator runs the eligibility draft on the winner
```

Use `plans/terapeak-capture-template.csv`. Its columns map one-to-one onto the six scoring dimensions,
so a filled row can be scored directly with no follow-up questions. Ten candidates per round.

**Do not accept a candidate described in prose.** "It sells well and looks cheap" cannot be scored.
Ask for the row. If a field is genuinely unavailable, mark it `unknown` rather than guessing — an
unknown is visible, a guess is not.

### On Terapeak's data depth

Secondary sources say three years. **Unverified, and not on the critical path.** The scoring above needs
only the **trailing 90 days**, so the method works at any depth eBay actually offers. Depth matters only
for the seasonality dimension, worth 10 points of 100.

Resolve it in about a minute once the account exists: open `Seller Hub → Research → Product Research`,
open the date-range selector, and note the furthest-back range offered. Record the real answer in the
verification ledger in `.agents/dropshipping-context.md` and stop treating it as an assumption.

## 7. From score to listing

When a candidate clears 75 and passes the eligibility gate:

1. **Specify it exactly** — not "phone stand" but "aluminium foldable phone stand, adjustable, matte black".
2. **Confirm CJ US stock** and note the real handling and delivery window.
3. **Compute the margin** from actual CJ cost plus real eBay fees, not estimates. Verify with
   `margin_stack.py` from `mardab96/ecommerce-claude-skills`.
4. **Build the title from Terapeak's own search terms**, not from what reads well.
5. **Produce images** within the truthfulness limits — enhancement of presentation only, never
   alteration of the product's real appearance.
6. **Set Buy It Now with immediate payment.** No auctions.
7. **State handling and delivery time precisely** — an eBay requirement and a salam condition at once.
